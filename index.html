Jett Medical is a family-owned durable medical equipment company proudly serving patients, healthcare providers, and communities across Alabama and surrounding areas. Since 2003, we have been committed to providing reliable medical equipment and personalized service to help meet the needs of every patient we serve. We offer a variety of products and services, including wheelchairs, walkers, bedside commodes, shower chairs, hospital beds, knee scooters, cold therapy units, orthopedic bracing, wound care supplies, and more. Our goal is to make the equipment process as simple and dependable as possible while providing quality care and excellent customer service. Explore our website to learn more about Jett Medical, the products and services we provide, and how our team can assist with your medical equipment needs.
