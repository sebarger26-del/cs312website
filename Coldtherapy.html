What is Cold Therapy?

Cold therapy, also known as cryotherapy, is a treatment that uses cold temperatures to reduce pain and inflammation. It is commonly used to treat sports injuries, muscle strains, and other conditions that cause pain and swelling.

Benefits of Cold Therapy

Cold therapy can provide several benefits, including:

  • Reducing pain and swelling
  • Decreasing muscle spasms
  • Improving recovery time
  • Enhancing athletic performance
