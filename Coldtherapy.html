What is Cold Therapy?

Cold therapy, also known as cryotherapy, is a treatment that involves the application of cold temperatures to the body to reduce pain and inflammation. It is commonly used in the treatment of sports injuries, muscle strains, and other conditions that cause pain and swelling.

Benefits of Cold Therapy

Cold therapy can provide several benefits, including:

  • Reducing pain and swelling
  • Decreasing muscle spasms
  • Improving recovery time
  • Enhancing athletic performance
